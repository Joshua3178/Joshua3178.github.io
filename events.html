2025-2026

UTAR Makers Club

Chairman, then auditor

I was chairman of the club in 2025 and auditor in 2026. During my 2025 term the club recruited more than 20 new members, and the workshop that year was attended by over 30 participants.

The workshop was built around games, hence the participants built and modified a short game with each sensor instead of stopping at a reading. Six activities were run.

Firstly, Fastest Wins paired light-dependent resistors (LDRs) with LEDs and a buzzer to form a two-player reaction game. Don't Move used a PIR motion sensor for a timed "Squid Game" challenge, the Laser and Light activity then used laser and LDR inputs for target and light-response games. In Sharp Shooter and Hand Cutter an ultrasonic sensor detected the target and triggered a servo. Don't Spill the Tea turned a water sensor, an LED and a buzzer into a spill alert. Lastly, gyroscope, tone and digital-sensor activities took the workshop past the basic blinking circuit.
