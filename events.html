2025-2026

UTAR Makers Club

Chairman, then auditor

Chairman in 2025 and auditor in 2026. The 2025 term recruited more than 20 new members and ran a workshop attended by over 30 participants.

The workshops made sensors playable. Participants built and modified short games instead of stopping at a sensor reading.

  • Fastest Wins: paired light-dependent resistors (LDRs), LEDs and a buzzer formed a two-player reaction game.
  • Don’t Move: a PIR motion sensor created a timed “Squid Game” challenge.
  • Laser and Light: laser and LDR inputs drove target and light-response activities.
  • Sharp Shooter and Hand Cutter: ultrasonic distance sensing triggered target detection and servo action.
  • Don’t Spill the Tea: a water sensor, LED and buzzer became a spill-alert game.
  • Motion and sound: gyroscope, tone and digital-sensor activities extended the workshop beyond basic blinking circuits.
