TEDxUTAR Kampar · 13 Sep 2025

TEDx speaker

On 13 September 2025 I spoke at TEDxUTAR Kampar, the theme that year was Resilience. My talk, Fake Optimism, True Resilience, was about mental health and the habit of pushing feelings down, I popped a balloon on stage to show where that pressure ends up. The second half gave a practical framework for facing fear, starting with writing the fear down on paper.

UTAR Makers Club · 2025 – 2026

Chairman, then auditor

I was chairman of the UTAR Makers Club in 2025 and auditor in 2026, the club recruited more than 20 new members during my term. Our workshop drew over 30 participants and was built around six sensor games, hence each sensor ended in a short game instead of a reading. Fastest Wins, for example, paired light sensors with LEDs and a buzzer for a two-player reaction game.

Debate · since 2016

Competitive debate

I joined debate at Chong Hwa in 2016 and won the Hua Yi Invitational English Debate Competition with Victoria School in 2019. At UTAR I moved to Chinese debate, at tournaments including NMMDC and CUDC, where I prepared competition cases through research and wrote questioning and speech material.
