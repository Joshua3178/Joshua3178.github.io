Final-year project · EEG-BCI

P300 EEG decoding for vision-guided robotic object retrieval.

A camera scene is converted into a four-object visual oddball interface. EEG flashes are decoded in one Transformer batch, accumulated into an object decision, rechecked against the camera frame and prepared for a robotic pick-and-drop action.

Signal and decision design

A discrete visual intention is accumulated before motion is prepared.

Visual protocol

Four-object stimulus interface

The camera identifies exactly four candidate objects. Their fixed identities are used through the visual flash sequence so the classifier result remains attached to the correct physical target.

P300 decoder

Transformer-based evidence scoring

The deployed route applies 0.1–20 Hz zero-phase filtering and common-average reference. A gated channel projection feeds a 192-dimensional Transformer with three encoder layers, four attention heads, 1024-dimensional feed-forward blocks, GELU and 0.20 dropout. The final object is the highest mean target-minus-non-target logit across its nine flashes.

Vision and robotic preparation

The target is checked in the camera frame before the arm moves.

Application boundary

Selection evidence and arm-command records
